## 15. Finiteness of $`V_M`$ (referee item B1) and the minor repairs (M1–M13)

The referee's verdict: given TR and [COMB](COMB.md), the
chain is correct except for one BLOCKING gap, B1, which says $`V_M`$ is never shown to be

New checks:

- a numerical check of the shape claim of Lemma G.

### 15.1 $`V_M`$ is finite (PROVED given TR; closes B1)

**Idea.**

- [CW12] Cor 6.3(2) describes $`P_1(\alpha)`$ as the closure of $`\{0, 1, \alpha\}`$ under additive
  decomposition, $`\mathrm{lh}_1`$ and bar. Cor 6.3(1) says it is finite: it is a pattern.
- $`V_M`$ is generated by the seeds $`()`$, $`(1)`$, $`M`$ and the operations "prefix sums, root
  segments, $`\mathrm{lh}_\Phi`$, anchor".
- The first three operations match decomposition and lh. So it suffices to show that
  **anchor never leaves a set of ordinals that is closed under decomposition, lh and
  bar.**
- This is not immediate. Take a non-epsilon $`N`$ whose last child absorbs the hi-block.
  Then $`\mathrm{bar}(o\, N) = e(C_k)`$ ([§11.3](PROOF-2.md)(b)) and $`o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N) \le e(C_k)`$, but equality is not proved
  (the referee's remark). Lemma H reaches $`o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N)`$ without it, using lh and bar
  together.

**Setting.** Call a set $`F`$ of ordinals below the core **good** if:

- $`0, 1 \in F`$;
- $`F`$ is closed under additive decomposition (the ANF components and the partial sums);
- $`F`$ is closed under lh ($`\mathrm{lh} = \mathrm{lh}_1`$ on $`(1, \mathrm{core})`$, [W07b] Thm 5.3; $`\mathrm{lh}(0) = 0`$ and $`\mathrm{lh}(1) = 1`$);
- $`F`$ is closed under bar on the additive principal elements of $`(1, \mathrm{core})`$.

For $`\alpha \in (1, \mathrm{core})`$, $`P_1(\alpha)`$ is good and finite ([CW12] Cor 6.3(1), (2); [§11.2](PROOF-2.md) for the $`\tau = 1`$
reading). If $`\alpha' \in P_1(\alpha)`$, then $`P_1(\alpha') \subseteq P_1(\alpha)`$, since $`P_1(\alpha')`$ is the closure of a subset.

**Fact BAR (PROVED from [CW12] Def 5.1, Lemmas 5.4, 5.10; [W07a] Lemmas 4.3, 4.9).**

<em>Statement.</em> Let $`\alpha = \omega^\zeta \in (1, \mathrm{core})`$, and let $`\zeta =_{\mathrm{CNF}} \omega^{\zeta_1} + \cdots + \omega^{\zeta_k}`$ with $`k \ge 2`$.
(Then $`\alpha \notin E`$ automatically: $`\omega^\zeta \in E`$ iff $`\zeta \in E`$, and an epsilon number is a single CNF
term.) Then

```math
\bar\alpha = \omega^{\zeta'}, \qquad \zeta' := \omega^{\zeta_1} + \cdots + \omega^{\zeta_{k-1}}.
```

<em>Proof.</em>

1. **The two cases are exhaustive.** Let $`(1 = \alpha_0, \ldots, \alpha_n = \alpha)`$ be the localization of $`\alpha`$.
   By [CW12] Lemma 5.4, the localization of $`\bar\alpha`$ is $`(\alpha_0 \ldots \alpha_{n-1})`$ or $`(\alpha_0 \ldots \alpha_{n-1}, \bar\alpha)`$. So
   $`\alpha_{n-1} \le \bar\alpha`$, and Lemma 5.10(2) or (3) applies.
2. **Case (2), $`\alpha_{n-1} \lt \bar\alpha`$.** It gives $`\bar\alpha = \omega^{\zeta'}`$ directly.
3. **Case (3), $`\alpha_{n-1} = \bar\alpha`$.** With $`k \ge 2`$ it forces $`k = 2`$ and $`\alpha_{n-1} = \zeta_1`$.
   - **If $`n - 1 \ge 1`$:** $`\alpha_{n-1}`$ is an intermediate element of the localization of the
     non-epsilon term $`\alpha`$.
     - The $`\Delta`$'s strictly decrease along a localization ([W07a] Lemma 4.9), and $`\alpha`$ has
       $`\Delta = 0`$.
     - So $`\alpha_{n-1}`$ has $`\Delta \gt 0`$, i.e. $`\alpha_{n-1} \in E`$ ([W07a] Lemma 4.3).
     - Then $`\zeta_1 \in E`$ and $`\omega^{\zeta'} = \omega^{\omega^{\zeta_1}} = \zeta_1 = \bar\alpha`$.
   - **If $`n - 1 = 0`$ (M11(a)):** then $`\zeta_1 = \alpha_0 = 1`$, so $`\zeta \in \{\omega+1, \omega \cdot 2\}`$ and
     $`\alpha \in \{\vartheta_0(\omega+1), \vartheta_0(\omega \cdot 2)\}`$.
     - For $`\vartheta_0(\omega+1)`$, Def 5.1's first case applies with $`\eta_0 = 1`$.
     - For $`\vartheta_0(\omega \cdot 2)`$, it applies with $`\eta' = \omega \lt \sup_{\sigma \lt \omega} \vartheta_0(\sigma) = \omega^\omega`$.
     - Both give $`\bar\alpha = \vartheta_0(\omega) = \omega^\omega \ne 1 = \alpha_0`$. So case (3) does not hold. ∎

**Lemma G (absorption shape; PROVED from [COMB](COMB.md) SC (G\*, Sib, (A)), Lemma 1.1, Lemma 2.1,
Lemma 2.4, [COMB](COMB.md) Lemma 4).**

<em>Statement.</em> Let $`Z \in R`$ be non-epsilon, with $`\mathrm{hi} := \mathrm{hi}(Z) \ne \emptyset`$, and let $`u`$ be its first $`y = 0`$
child. Suppose $`o((0, \mathrm{hi})) \lt o(\mathrm{sh}\, u)`$, i.e. $`(0, \mathrm{hi})`$ is absorbed when $`\log Z`$ is formed. Then:

- $`\mathrm{ch}(u) = \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} R`$ as a sequence of blocks, with $`R \ne \emptyset`$;
- every element of $`R`$ has $`y = 0`$.

Hence $`\mathrm{sh}\, u`$ is non-epsilon, $`\mathrm{hi}(\mathrm{sh}\, u) = \mathrm{hi}`$, and $`\mathrm{lo}(\mathrm{sh}\, u) = R`$. (Checked numerically on 7,513 absorbed
cases.)

<em>Proof.</em>

1. **Order of the children.** By Sib, $`\mathrm{ch}(Z) = \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} \mathrm{lo}(Z)`$: a block with head $`y = 1`$ is $`\gt_p`$
   a block with head $`y = 0`$.
2. **Upper bound from G\*.** $`u`$ is descending ($`y_u = 0 \le 0`$), and $`v(u)`$ is the root. G\*
   gives $`T(u) \lt_p T(\mathrm{root})`$, i.e. $`\mathrm{ch}(u) \lt_{\mathrm{lex}} \mathrm{ch}(Z) = \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} \mathrm{lo}(Z)`$. The block comparison is
   the argument of Lemma 2.1 applied one level down (at depth 1), not Lemma 2.1 itself.
3. **Lower bound from absorption.**
   - $`(0, \mathrm{hi})`$ is standard (an iterated anchor, Lemma 2.4), and $`\mathrm{sh}\, u`$ is standard ([COMB](COMB.md)
     Lemma 4).
   - By Lemma 1.1, absorption means $`(0, \mathrm{hi}) \lt_p \mathrm{sh}\, u`$, i.e. $`\mathrm{hi} \lt_{\mathrm{lex}} \mathrm{ch}(u)`$.
4. **$`\mathrm{hi}`$ is a proper prefix of $`\mathrm{ch}(u)`$.**
   - Suppose $`\mathrm{ch}(u)`$ and $`\mathrm{hi}`$ first differ at a position $`i`$ where both are defined. Then the
     comparisons with $`\mathrm{hi}`$ and with $`\mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} \mathrm{lo}(Z)`$ are both decided at $`i`$ with the same
     outcome, which contradicts steps 2 and 3.
   - If $`\mathrm{ch}(u)`$ were a prefix of $`\mathrm{hi}`$, then $`\mathrm{ch}(u) \le_{\mathrm{lex}} \mathrm{hi}`$, which contradicts step 3.
   - So $`\mathrm{ch}(u) = \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} R`$ with $`R \ne \emptyset`$.
5. **The $`y`$-values in $`R`$.**
   - Cancelling the common prefix in step 2 gives $`R \lt_{\mathrm{lex}} \mathrm{lo}(Z)`$. So the first element $`R_1`$
     is $`\le_p \mathrm{lo}(Z)_1 = u`$ as blocks, and $`u`$ has head $`y = 0`$. So $`R_1`$ has $`y = 0`$.
   - The children of $`u`$ have $`y \le 1`$ by (A). By Sib a $`y = 1`$ block cannot follow a $`y = 0`$
     block. So all of $`R`$ has $`y = 0`$. ∎

**Lemma AF (anchor stays in a good set; PROVED from Fact BAR, R, $`\mathrm{Bar}_T`$ + TR, and
Lemma H).**

<em>Statement.</em> Let $`F`$ be good, $`N \in R`$ with $`k \ge 2`$ children $`C_1 \ldots C_k`$, and $`o(N) \in F`$. Then
$`o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N) \in F`$.

<em>Proof.</em>

- **(i) $`N`$ epsilon.** $`\mathrm{Bar}_T`$ ([§14.2](PROOF-3.md)) and TR give $`\mathrm{bar}(o\, N) = o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N)`$.
- **(ii) $`N`$ non-epsilon, and $`C_{k-1}`$ has $`y = 0`$.**
  - $`\log N = \log(\mathrm{anchor}\, N) \mathbin{+\!\!+} (\mathrm{sh}\, C_k)`$, with no absorption.
    - $`\log`$ is formed by successive CNF additions, so $`\log N = \log(\mathrm{anchor}\, N) + \mathrm{sh}\, C_k`$.
    - The last root segment of $`\log(\mathrm{anchor}\, N)`$ is $`\mathrm{sh}\, C_{k-1}`$, because the last appended
      term is never removed.
    - $`\mathrm{sh}\, C_k \le_p \mathrm{sh}\, C_{k-1}`$ (Sib), so $`o(C_k) \le o(C_{k-1})`$ (Lemma 1.1), and adding $`C_k`$
      removes nothing.
  - So $`\zeta = o(\log N)`$ has at least 2 CNF terms, and $`\zeta' = o(\log \mathrm{anchor}\, N)`$.
  - $`\mathrm{anchor}\, N`$ is non-epsilon (its last child $`C_{k-1}`$ has $`y = 0`$) and $`\ne 1`$.
  - Fact BAR and Lemma R(ii) give $`\mathrm{bar}(o\, N) = \omega^{o(\log \mathrm{anchor}\, N)} = o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N)`$.
- **(iii) $`N`$ non-epsilon, and $`C_{k-1}`$ has $`y = 1`$.**
  - Then $`C_1 \ldots C_{k-1}`$ all have $`y = 1`$, since hi comes before lo. So $`C_k`$ is the only $`y = 0`$
    child, and $`\mathrm{anchor}\, N = (0, \mathrm{hi}\, N)`$.
  - Lemma H with $`Z = N`$ and $`B = \mathrm{hi}\, N`$ gives $`o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N) \in F`$. ∎

(Case (iii) contains the referee's case: when $`C_k`$ absorbs $`(0, \mathrm{hi}\, N)`$, $`\mathrm{bar}(o\, N) = e(C_k)`$
([§11.3](PROOF-2.md)(b)), and $`o(\mathrm{anchor}\, N) = e(C_k)`$ is not claimed.)

**Lemma H (PROVED by induction on the number of columns; uses Lemma AF(i), (ii), Fact
BAR, Lemma G, Lemma 4.2, R, [COMB](COMB.md) Lemma 4, Lemma 2.4).**

<em>Statement.</em> Let $`F`$ be good, $`Z \in R`$ with $`o(Z) \in F`$, and let $`B`$ be a nonempty initial segment
of $`\mathrm{ch}(Z)`$ whose members all have $`y = 1`$. Then $`o((0, B)) \in F`$. ($`(0, B)`$ is standard: it is an
iterated anchor of $`Z`$, or $`Z`$ itself, by Lemma 2.4.)

<em>Proof.</em>

**Case 1: $`Z`$ epsilon.**

- All children have $`y = 1`$ ([COMB](COMB.md) T3).
- Let $`Z = Z_0`$, $`Z_1 = \mathrm{anchor}\, Z_0`$, …, $`Z_t = (0, B)`$. Each $`Z_i`$ with $`i \lt t`$ is a standard epsilon
  root with $`\ge 2`$ children.
- By AF(i), $`o(Z_{i+1}) = \mathrm{bar}(o\, Z_i) \in F`$. So $`o((0, B)) \in F`$.

**Case 2: $`Z`$ non-epsilon.** Write $`\mathrm{ch}(Z) = \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} (\mathrm{lo}_1 \ldots \mathrm{lo}_m)`$ with $`m \ge 1`$. $`B \subseteq \mathrm{hi}`$, so $`\mathrm{hi} \ne \emptyset`$.
Put $`H_0 := (0, \mathrm{hi})`$, a standard epsilon root. By Case 1 applied to $`H_0`$ ($`B`$ is an initial
segment of $`\mathrm{hi} = \mathrm{ch}\, H_0`$), it suffices to show $`o(H_0) \in F`$.

- **2a. Reduce to one $`y = 0`$ child.**
  - For $`m \ge 2`$, the next-to-last child of $`Z`$ has $`y = 0`$. AF(ii) applies and gives
    $`o(\mathrm{anchor}\, Z) \in F`$. AF(ii) does not use Lemma H.
  - Repeating this $`m - 1`$ times gives $`o(Z_1) \in F`$ for $`Z_1 := (0, \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} [\mathrm{lo}_1])`$.
  - Put $`u := \mathrm{lo}_1`$.
- **2b. $`(0, \mathrm{hi})`$ is not absorbed by $`u`$**, i.e. $`o(H_0) \ge o(\mathrm{sh}\, u)`$.
  - Then $`\log Z_1 = (H_0, \mathrm{sh}\, u)`$ and $`o(Z_1) = \omega^{o(H_0) + o(u)}`$ (Lemma R), with two CNF terms.
  - Fact BAR gives $`\mathrm{bar}(o\, Z_1) = \omega^{o(H_0)} = o(H_0)`$, since $`H_0`$ is epsilon. So $`o(H_0) \in F`$.
- **2c. $`(0, \mathrm{hi})`$ is absorbed by $`u`$.**
  1. **Shape.** Then $`\log Z_1 = (\mathrm{sh}\, u)`$. By Lemma G, $`S := \mathrm{sh}\, u`$ is non-epsilon, with
     $`\mathrm{hi}(S) = \mathrm{hi}`$ and $`\mathrm{lo}(S) = R = (R_1, \ldots) \ne \emptyset`$.
  2. **The reach.** Lemma 4.2 for $`Z_1`$ gives
     $`\mathrm{lh}(o\, Z_1) = o(Z_1) + o(\lambda Z_1) = o(Z_1) + o(\log S)`$, since $`\lambda(Z_1) = \mathcal{L}(u) = \log S`$.
  3. **Decomposition.** This value is in $`F`$. Its ANF components are $`o(Z_1)`$ and the values
     of the root segments of $`\log S`$ (each $`\le o(S) \lt o(Z_1)`$). So $`o(t) \in F`$ for every root
     segment $`t`$ of $`\log S`$.
  4. **Where $`H_0`$ goes.** $`\log S = \mathrm{add}((H_0), R_1, \ldots)`$.
     - If $`H_0`$ is not absorbed in $`\log S`$, it is the first root segment, so $`o(H_0) \in F`$.
     - Otherwise the first root segment is $`S_2 := \mathrm{sh}\, R_1`$, so $`o(S_2) \in F`$.
  5. **In the second case, recurse.**
     - $`S_2`$ is standard ([COMB](COMB.md) Lemma 4).
     - Lemma G applied to the non-epsilon $`S`$ ($`\mathrm{hi}(S) = \mathrm{hi} \ne \emptyset`$, first $`y = 0`$ child $`R_1`$,
       absorbed) gives $`\mathrm{ch}(S_2) = \mathrm{hi} \mathbin{+\!\!+} R_2`$. So $`B`$ is a nonempty initial segment of $`\mathrm{ch}(S_2)`$
       with all members of $`y = 1`$.
     - $`S_2`$ is a proper subtree of $`u`$, which is a proper subtree of $`Z`$. So $`S_2`$ has fewer
       columns than $`Z`$.
     - The induction hypothesis gives $`o((0, B)) \in F`$ directly. ∎

**Theorem VF (PROVED given TR; closes B1).** For every $`M \in \mathrm{Std}`$ with $`o(M) \ge 2`$:

```math
o[V_M] \subseteq P_1(o(M)).
```

In particular $`V_M`$ is finite, with $`\lvert V_M \rvert \le \lvert P_1(o(M)) \rvert`$. For $`M = (0,0)`$, $`V_M = \{(), (1)\}`$.

<em>Proof.</em>

1. **The set $`U`$.** Let $`F := P_1(o(M))`$, which is good and finite. Put
   $`U := \{x \text{ a node} : o(x) \in F\}`$.
2. **$`U`$ contains the seeds.** $`o(()) = 0`$, $`o((1)) = 1`$ and $`o(M)`$ are all in $`F`$.
3. **$`U`$ is closed under root segments and prefix sums.** The $`o`$-values are the ANF
   components and partial sums of $`o(x)`$ (Lemma 2.2, Cor 2.3), and $`F`$ is closed under them.
4. **$`U`$ is closed under $`\mathrm{lh}_\Phi`$.** For a one-term $`x \in U`$, $`o(\mathrm{lh}_\Phi x) = \mathrm{lh}(o\, x)`$ by L, and $`F`$ is
   closed under lh.
5. **$`U`$ is closed under anchor.** This is Lemma AF.
6. **Conclusion.** $`V_M`$ is the least set that contains the seeds and is closed under these
   operations. So $`V_M \subseteq U`$. $`o`$ is injective (Lemma 1.1), so $`\lvert V_M \rvert \le \lvert F \rvert \lt \infty`$. ∎

**Cor VF (PROVED given TR).** $`o[V_M] = P_1(o(M))`$ for every $`M`$ with $`o(M) \ge 2`$.

- $`\subseteq`$ is Thm VF.
- $`\supseteq`$ is [§11.2](PROOF-2.md) step 3, which uses bar-closure and Thm 6.2. It applies because $`V_M`$ is now
  known to be finite.

So $`\Phi(M)`$ is exactly Carlson–Wilken's $`P_1(o(M))`$, realized on pair sequences.

**No circularity.**

- Thm VF uses L, $`\mathrm{Bar}_T`$, TR, R, Lemma 4.2, [COMB](COMB.md) (SC, Lemma 4, Lemma 2.4) and [CW12]
  Cor 6.3 / Lemma 5.4 / Lemma 5.10.
- None of these uses the finiteness of $`V_M`$.
  - L is proved on the $`\mathrm{lh}_\Phi`$ call tree (Prop 4.3, [COMB](COMB.md) T).
  - E2 is proved on the $`\vartheta`$-side ([§14.3](PROOF-3.md)).
- Lemma 5.1, Cor 5.2 and Thm 11.1 come after Thm VF.

**Evidence.**

- A numerical check compares $`\mathcal{T}[V_M]`$ with $`P_1(\mathcal{T}M)`$, computed purely
  in $`T^1`$ (closure under decomposition, $`\mathrm{lh}^1`$ and bar).
  - They are **equal** on all 231,105 matrices of three generated test sets (dense sets with maximal $`y = 1, 2, 3`$): 49,702 + 148,435 + 32,968.
  - Max $`\lvert V_M \rvert = 19`$. There were no cap hits.
  - This checks both Thm VF and Cor VF.
- Lemma G's shape claim was checked on 84,201 non-epsilon roots with $`\mathrm{hi}, \mathrm{lo} \ne \emptyset`$, of
  which 7,513 absorb. 0 failures.
- A check run by the referee found max $`\lvert V \rvert = 19`$ on 181,405 matrices.

**Lean.** Lemma AF is `lemmaAF` in [Main/VF.lean](../Main/VF.lean) (the non-epsilon part is `lemmaAF_noneps` in [Main/AF.lean](../Main/AF.lean), the epsilon part is $`\mathrm{Bar}_T`$), and Theorem VF and the finiteness of $`V_M`$ are `thmVF` and `finite_V` there.

### 15.2 The minor items

**Lemma N⁺ (PROVED from the definition of $`\mathcal{T}`$; closes M10).**

<em>Statement.</em> Let $`N = (0, A)`$ be a standard epsilon root, $`A = (H_1 \ldots H_r)`$, $`W = H_r`$,
$`\alpha = \mathcal{T}(N) = \vartheta_0(\Delta+\eta)`$. Let $`W' :=`$ $`W`$ without its $`y = 0`$ children, and $`N^+ := (0, A \mathbin{+\!\!+} [W'])`$.
Then

```math
\mathcal{T}(N^+) = \vartheta_0(\Delta + \eta + 1) = \alpha^+ \qquad (\text{[W07a] p.142: } \alpha^+ := \vartheta^\tau(\Delta + \eta + 1)).
```

The same statement for the anchor $`a`$ with last child $`H_{k-1}`$ gives $`\mathcal{T}(a^+) = e_p^+`$ ([§13.3](PROOF-3.md) (b)).

<em>Proof.</em>

1. **The claim to show.** It suffices that $`X_{W'} = \Delta`$, i.e. $`D(W') = \Delta`$ and $`\rho_{W'} = 0`$.
   Then:
   - $`W'`$ joins $`N`$'s last run, whose $`D`$-part is $`\Delta`$;
   - the prefix before the run and the test "$`e_p \gt \Delta^\star`$" are unchanged;
   - the coefficient becomes $`c^+ = c + \omega^0 = c + 1`$.

   Hence $`\eta^+ = [e_p +](-1 + c + 1) = [e_p +]((-1 + c) + 1) = \eta + 1`$. (For $`c = n`$ finite,
   $`-1 + (n+1) = n = (n-1) + 1`$. For $`c`$ infinite, $`-1 + c + 1 = c + 1`$.)
2. **Recall** ([§12.5](PROOF-2.md)) that $`X_W = \log_\omega \mathcal{T}_1(W) = D(W) + \rho_W`$, with $`D(W) = \Delta`$, since $`W`$ is in the
   last run.
3. **The four shapes of $`W`$** ([COMB](COMB.md) T4: the $`D`$'s come before the $`E`$'s).
   - **$`W`$ epsilon at level 1** (all children have $`y = 2`$).
     - $`W' = W`$ and $`X_W = \mathcal{T}_1(W) = \Delta`$, $`\rho_W = 0`$.
   - **$`W`$ has $`y = 1`$ $`E`$-children.**
     - $`\mathcal{T}_1(W') = \omega^{Z'}`$ with $`Z' = [\mathcal{T}_1(W|_p) \text{ or } \Omega] + \sum_{y(E)=1} \mathcal{T}_1(E)`$.
     - All summands of $`Z'`$ are $`\ge \Omega_1`$, so $`X_{W'} = Z'`$ and $`\rho_{W'} = 0`$.
     - $`Z'`$ is exactly the part of level $`\ge 1`$ of $`X_W = Z' + \rho_W`$. No absorption happens,
       since $`\rho_W \lt \Omega_1`$. So $`Z' = D(W) = \Delta`$.
   - **$`W`$ has $`D`$-children and only $`y = 0`$ $`E`$-children.**
     - $`W' = W|_p`$ is epsilon at level 1, so $`X_{W'} = \mathcal{T}_1(W|_p) = Z_D = D(W) = \Delta`$.
   - **$`W`$ has no child of $`y \ge 1`$.**
     - $`W' = (1)`$, $`\mathcal{T}_1(W') = \vartheta_1(0) = \Omega_1 = \omega^{\Omega_1}`$ at level 1, so $`X_{W'} = \Omega_1`$.
     - Also $`X_W = \Omega + \rho_W`$, so $`D(W) = \Omega = \Delta`$. ∎

(Checked numerically on 75,328 cases.)

## Review history

The text was checked by an independent referee in two rounds. The referee's scale: FATAL
(the chain is wrong), BLOCKING (a step the Main Theorem needs has no proof), MINOR (a local
gap, a wrong citation, or an inconsistency that is easy to repair). The referee took [TR]
and [COMB] as given; they were reviewed separately (see the Review history of [TR](TR.md)
and of [COMB](COMB.md)). The referee compared the cited statements with the texts of
[C01], [W07a], [W07b] and [CW12], and with the Lean facts in [Rank.lean](../Rank.lean).

**First review.** No FATAL error. The chain L → Lemma 5.1 → bar-closure → Thm 11.1 →
Cor 7.2 was found correct given [TR] and [COMB], with one BLOCKING gap and thirteen MINOR
items:

- **B1 (BLOCKING).** $`V_M`$ was not shown to be finite. $`\Phi(M)`$ is a pattern in the sense
  of [C01] Def 4.2, and [C01] Thm 5.9, [CW12] Thm 6.2 and Lemma 6.1 apply to it, only if
  $`V_M`$ is finite. (The referee's own numerical check found at most 19 nodes in
  $`V_M`$ on 181,405 matrices.) Repaired by Thm VF and Cor VF (§15.1).
- **M1.** Thm 7.1 still cited the unfinished Route B.
- **M2.** Lemma 6.1 had no proof.
- **M3.** Thm 11.1 did not cover $`M = (0,0)`$.
- **M4.** [CW12] §6 depends on [W07c], which was not consulted; the reading of the
  1-relativized notions for $`\tau = 1`$ had to be stated.
- **M5.** Lemma CI, epsilon case $`m = 1`$: the translation $`t^\alpha_\tau`$ needs its domain
  condition.
- **M6.** Two citation gaps in the proof of E2 ([§14.3](PROOF-3.md)).
- **M7.** The order of the induction in Prop 4.3 and E2 was not stated.
- **M8.** Lemma NS: one sub-case was missing.
- **M9.** $`\mathrm{Bar}_T`$ (a) used NS without citing it.
- **M10.** $`\mathcal{T}(N^+) = \alpha^+`$ and $`\mathcal{T}(a^+) = e_p^+`$ were checked
  numerically, not proved.
- **M11.** Small omissions in [§11.3](PROOF-2.md).
- **M12.** The justification of Prop 7.3 was wrong.
- **M13.** The identification C3 rests on external results.

The repair of each item, and where it is, is in the table at the end of §15.2.

**Second review.** B1 is closed, and M1–M13 are repaired. No FATAL or BLOCKING issue
remains. Three editorial items were found: N1 (the equality of §15.1 had two names, "Cor VF"
and "Cor VF′"), N2 (stale notes that [TR] was still under review), and N3 (Lemma G, step 2,
cited Lemma 2.1 where its analogue one level down is meant). The present text uses the name
Cor VF only, calls [TR] reviewed and accepted, and states the one-level-down reading in
Lemma G. The referee's verdict: given the accepted [TR] and [COMB] and the cited
literature, the Main Theorem $`\iota(\Phi(M)) = o(M)`$ is proved, including both halves of
the conjecture; Cor 7.2(b)'s "$`\mathrm{Core} \setminus \{0\}`$" is proved up to the outside
assumption M13.
