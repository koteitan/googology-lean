- Wilken's claim (A glimpse of Σ₃-elementarity, 2020, p. 421; repeated in Pure Σ₂-elementarity beyond the core, 2021, §1): "the segment of countable ordinals denoted by the Skolem-hull notation system derived from the first ω-many weakly inaccessible cardinals covers (the domain of) Core(R₂⁺)". R₂⁺ = (Ord; 0, +, ≤, ≤₁, ≤₂), and its core is the union of the least realizations of its finite patterns. Statement, results and experiments: [Trans/PoR/InaccPsi](Googology/Trans/PoR/InaccPsi/README.md). Our reading: the core equals the set of countable values of the [InaccPsi](Googology/Notation/InaccPsi/README.md) terms whose collapse arguments are below I_ω (the supremum of the inaccessibles), that is the ordinals below ψ_{Ω₁}(I_ω). R₂⁺ has two definitions: Carlson's by coverings (R₂^C, used first) and Wilken's by Σₙ-elementarity (R₂^S). Wilken's points υ_ι: υ₁ = ψ_{Ω₁}(Ω_ω) is the core of R₁⁺, and υ_{ι+1} is the same construction over υ_ι. A chain of length n is n additive principal numbers that are pairwise ≤₂. Done: the countable values form an initial segment (Lean); in R₂^C the core is the supremum of the least chains (Theorem CC, refereed); below υ_{ω·ω} the claim holds in both R₂^C and R₂^S: every ordinal there is in the core and is the value of a term (refereed; the step υ_{ξ+1} ≤ ψ_{Ω₁}(A + θ) is proved through the simultaneously defined ϑ-functions of Weiermann–Wilken 2011, refereed twice, and its InaccPsi side is in Lean); the exact names υ_{1+η} = ψ_{Ω₁}(Ω_ω + θ·η) for every η < Γ₀, where θ = ψ_{Ω₂}(Ω_ω) (Theorem T: the upper half refereed twice, the lower half once; it uses a map from InaccPsi values into Wilken's ϑ-system; so υ₁ = ψ_{Ω₁}(Ω_ω) needs no proof-theoretic citation; the induction up to η = ω·ω is in Lean); every pattern without a chain of length 3 lies below the least ≤₁-predecessor of the least chain of length 3 (Lemma DOM₂, refereed); finite-set tests for ≤₁ and ≤₂ in R₂^S (refereed)
